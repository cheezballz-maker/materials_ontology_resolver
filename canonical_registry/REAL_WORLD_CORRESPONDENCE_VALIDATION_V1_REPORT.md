# Real-World Correspondence Validation v1

## Executive conclusion

The diagnostic interface now reports **chemical graph correspondence, source-identity qualification, upstream independence and admission separately**. It used 31 real reference cases from 21 actual cached source records and separately acquired NIST molecular files. Thirty cases have documentary labels suitable for the declared represented-graph scope; one unresolved reference convention is excluded. Results agree with **29/30 documentary labels**, with **zero false equivalences and zero false differences against those labels** and one unnecessary abstention on a known stereocenter in a partially specified molecule.

This is provisional validation of the documented structural-comparison scope. It is **not expert-reviewed chemical certification, independently corroborated canonical resolution or an estimate of population precision**. No qualified expert assessed the corpus. Documentary grounding is independent of resolver predictions, while ultimate source-data independence generally remains unknown. Review-pending/unlabelled cases are not scored as confirmed correct or incorrect.

All 324 unit/integration tests, 55 retained correspondence controls and eight historical safety cases passed. The ten prior PubChem–ChEBI pairs now expose 8 represented-graph equivalences, 1 difference and 1 unresolved comparison; no independent corroboration is inferred. Two existing registry entries were retrieved and chemically matched, with known shared PubChem ancestry. All admission recommendations are withheld under the evidence supplied. Production code, registry, ontology and provider policy remain unchanged.

## Three decisions and implementation reuse

The new code is confined to [real_world_validation](real_world_validation/). The default-off [assessment interface](real_world_validation/correspondence_assessment.py) reuses existing source adapters, source qualification, candidate qualification, identity reconciliation, dimension comparisons, isolated isotope correction and the unchanged admission-policy function. It introduces no chemistry parser, chemical identity key, provider adapter or registry schema.

| Layer | Meaning | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Represented structure | CHEMICALLY_EQUIVALENT / DIFFERENT / UNRESOLVED for selected molecular graphs and explicit composition. | Source-name/material intent, upstream independence or production EXACT. |
| Source identity | Qualified binding and sufficiently specified source constraints; conflicts and unresolved claims retained. | Independent external corroboration. |
| Evidence independence | INDEPENDENTLY_ESTABLISHED / KNOWN_DEPENDENT / INDEPENDENCE_UNESTABLISHED from auditable ancestry. | Chemistry solely through database membership or matching keys. |
| Admission | Existing policy exercised with the evidence actually supplied. | Permission to create a Substance or bypass required checks. |

The prior independence-gated correspondence result remains visible in each output and is not modified. The thin interface exposes its computed chemistry separately. Represented-graph comparison has explicit composition scope, so an unclear salt/physical-phase interpretation cannot conceal an otherwise valid comparison of the supplied components. The original incoming concept retains its stricter source scope. Multiple source structures are not resolved by selecting the first.

Derived identifiers are checked in full candidate/source-identity qualification, but are not prerequisites for reporting what a selected valid molecular file depicts. A real NIST inconsistency exposed that distinction during evaluation; the final interface preserves the mismatch while allowing the graph comparison. No comparator or chemical acceptance rule was changed.

## Corpus construction, provenance and freezing

The corpus contains actual saved PubChem responses, curated ChEBI records and NIST Chemistry WebBook molecular files. Candidates are read from their own original reference fields/files, never copied from an incoming normalized graph. Expected labels were documented before implementing/executing this interface, using explicit chemical identity descriptions, molecular diagrams, named stereo states and curated acid/base definitions. They were not generated by the resolver or copied from its old results.

Selection is purposive rather than random. Several common compounds appeared in earlier experiments; this is not a fresh blind holdout. The 9 development/reference and 22 frozen evaluation cases are grouped by chemical identity family, keeping a source identity out of both partitions. All labels and provenance were public during development. The previously requested cefpiramide challenge was already known and is explicitly nonblind.

[reference_cases.json](real_world_validation/reference_corpus/reference_cases.json) records every original source/reference, structures, scopes, expected chemical and independence results, applicable dimensions, documentary justification and review status. [reference_adjudication.json](real_world_validation/reference_corpus/reference_adjudication.json) retains frozen expectations and empty label-revision histories; [provenance_records.json](real_world_validation/reference_corpus/provenance_records.json) contains original field URLs, file paths, hashes, timestamps and contributor metadata. Original molecular files and source identifiers remain available for human review.

Thirty cases are classified INDEPENDENT_DOCUMENTARY_GROUNDING with documentary annotation completed, without qualified expert review. This means their graph-level labels have explicit external documentary grounds; it does not certify empirical accuracy of every database assertion. RW29 is REVIEW_PENDING and excluded from accuracy. There are zero QUALIFIED_EXPERT_REVIEWED cases and zero independently corroborated accuracy cases.

Public validation acquisition was separately logged in [retrieval_manifest.json](real_world_validation/validation_material/retrieval_manifest.json). It comprises 22 fixed NIST page requests and 19 linked molecular files: 19 pages provide usable identity fields, while three initial accession attempts supplied no qualified identity fields and were not used as molecular references. The benzene-d6 accession was corrected before corpus freeze; failed pages and the initial manifest remain. No production resolver client or provider-query budget was used. Downloading a reference separately does not prove upstream independence.

Representative primary material includes NIST [ethanol](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C64175&Units=SI), [dimethyl ether](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C115106&Units=SI), [deuterated acetone](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C666524&Units=SI), [deuterium oxide](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7789200&Units=SI), [R-butanol](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C14898794&Units=SI) and [sodium acetate trihydrate](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C6131904&Units=SI). Chemical boundaries are grounded in [IUPAC isotope definitions](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P8.html), [stereo specification](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P9.html#P-93.1), [ions](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P7.html) and [indicated hydrogen](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P1.html#P-14.7). References ground the labels; automated parsing is not expert review.

## False-match analysis and abstention

The sole scored disagreement is **RW30**, PubChem 50989996 versus 820607. The source fixes the OH-bearing indanol C1 as S while leaving C2 unspecified; the reference fixes C1 as R and C2 as R. The known C1 mismatch excludes the reference regardless of source C2. The retained comparator marks the whole stereo dimension UNKNOWN whenever a center is unspecified, losing this valid exclusion. This is a conservative comparator coverage limitation, not a false equivalence or false rejection. No molecule-specific exception or grammar change was made.

RW23/RW24 correctly remain unresolved because the reference is consistent with one of the source’s unspecified stereo possibilities. RW25 is a polymer with no qualified discrete molecular structure. RW29 has an unresolved reference representation convention. Its NIST sodium-acetate molfile uses an O–Na bond while its InChI projects ionic components; the incoming graph is already ionic. The existing coordination capability guard prevents forcing equivalence or a Substance-level difference. Reference review remains pending.

All unresolved and withheld decisions are classified in [failure_taxonomy.json](real_world_validation/evaluation/failure_taxonomy.json) and [abstention_analysis.json](real_world_validation/evaluation/abstention_analysis.json), distinguishing missing evidence, specificity, candidate inconsistency, representation/capability limits, provenance and admission requirements. No actual false chemical equivalence was found in the scored documentary population; this does not prove its absence in unsupported chemistry.

## Reference quality finding and minimal interface correction

RW12/RW17 exposed a record-field disagreement in the NIST butanol references. Under installed RDKit decoding, the R and S molecular files agree with their published R/S names, while the pages’ Standard InChI/InChIKey correspond to the opposite decoded configurations. The atom priorities/wedge interpretation also support the molecular-file configurations. No cause or expert-certified correction to NIST data is asserted. The affected fields are preserved in [reference_quality_findings.json](real_world_validation/evaluation/reference_quality_findings.json).

Initially the thin interface let those derived identifier inconsistencies conceal graph comparison, causing two additional abstentions. The scope correction now reports selected-file graph equivalence/difference and retains the identifier disagreement in full candidate/source-identity qualification. RW12/RW17 therefore remain withheld at the source-identity/admission layers. No published data, expected label, comparator, production rule or reference identity was rewritten to make tests pass.

The initial scope is reproducible from its exact recorded source hash, with results stored separately. The first result files had not been backed up before final replay, so they are explicitly labelled reproductions rather than original untouched outputs. [Revision history](real_world_validation/evaluation/implementation_revision_history.json) and [initial-scope reproduction](real_world_validation/evaluation/initial_scope_reproduction.json) document the change. The final public evaluation is not blinded.

## Recommended next development task

Implement and independently test **bounded comparison of already specified stereocenters in partially specified graphs**. RW30 demonstrates a general, source-independent exclusion that the current whole-dimension UNKNOWN rule misses. Preserve uncertainty at other centers and admit nothing merely because one center agrees. This requires atom correspondence and stereo evidence, not a broader nomenclature parser or a molecule-specific exception.

Before production integration, obtain qualified review of this corpus, resolve the two NIST record-field discrepancies and the sodium-bond representation convention, then acquire auditable original-source provenance for a small real positive-corroboration set. Expand the unsupported isotope-unknown-name, true incoming-conflict and charged-resonance categories only when actual reference evidence exists. The present evidence supports the tested represented-graph scope, not comprehensive chemical resolution or production readiness.
